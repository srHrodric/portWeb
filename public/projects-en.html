01 — INDEPENDENT PROJECT / CASE STUDY

ChurchLog — Management SaaS.

Web management platform for churches and religious organizations. Its central challenge is organizing multiple branches and processes in an integrated application while keeping each organization's information separate and secure.

CHURCHLOGCL.MANAGEMENT · PROCESSES · TECHNOLOGY
NODE.JSEXPRESSPOSTGRESQLSEQUELIZEEJSSOCKET.IO

Architecture and purpose

Multi-tenant SaaS application for ministerial and administrative management. Its structure covers organizations, branches, members and users, with organization-level data isolation and role-based access control.

The system is organized into administrative and operational modules, including areas such as administration, finance, school, departments, library and system management. The goal is to centralize processes and information while respecting each organization's specific needs.

Technical decisions and challenges

  • Multi-tenant architecture and organization-level data isolation.
  • Relational data modeling with PostgreSQL and Sequelize.
  • Authentication, sessions, authorization and access levels.
  • Modular organization of business rules and administrative workflows.
  • APIs and integrations, real-time communication and scheduled tasks.
  • Application infrastructure with Node.js, Express, Nginx and PM2.

The technologies and components listed reflect the project's implementation and technical planning; features still evolving are not presented as completed deliveries.
